# Security

Zom's HaRM keeps saved logins, 2FA codes and keys, so security problems matter. Thank you for looking.

## Reporting a problem

Please **don't open a public issue** for a security problem. Tell us privately instead:

- Email **info@zombeatzmedia.com** with "HaRM security" in the subject, or
- On GitHub, use **Security > Report a vulnerability** on this repository.

Say what you found, the HaRM version (**Settings > About**), and how to make it happen. You'll get an answer within a week. Once it's fixed, the release notes say so, and credit you if you'd like.

## Which versions get fixes

Only the newest release. Updating is safe: your data is kept in its own folder (see [Installing and updating](https://docs.zombeatzmedia.com/harm/installing)).

## How HaRM protects your data

[Security and privacy](https://docs.zombeatzmedia.com/harm/security) in the guide explains it: logins are encrypted with your master password, HaRM locks itself after a while without use, and nothing is sent to the internet unless you choose an online AI service.
